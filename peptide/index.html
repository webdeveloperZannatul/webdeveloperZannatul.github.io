Everything you need to know about peptide research

All of our peptides maintain 99%+ identity purity, verified through independent third-party HPLC testing. Every batch comes with a Certificate of Analysis (CoA) that you can review before or after purchase.

A CoA is an official document from an accredited laboratory that verifies the identity, purity, and composition of a peptide batch. It typically includes HPLC results, mass spectrometry data, and other analytical measurements.

Amino H2O is our premium bacteriostatic water designed specifically for reconstituting lyophilized peptides in a research setting.

Lyophilized peptides should be stored in a cool, dry place. For long-term storage, we recommend refrigeration at 2-8°C or freezing at -20°C.

When properly stored, lyophilized peptides are typically stable for 12-24 months. Once reconstituted, the solution should be used within a shorter timeframe.

We ship orders within 1 business day. Lyophilized peptides are stable at room temperature for transit. Cold shipping is available upon request for reconstituted products.
